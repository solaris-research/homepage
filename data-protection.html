Data protection

This page explains how the Solaris Research website processes personal data.

Scope

This website is an informational site for Solaris Research. It has no user accounts, comment areas, advertising or analytics.

Hosting and technical data

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the USA. When you visit, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and time of access to deliver the site and keep it secure. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Fonts

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, a service of Google LLC in the USA. To deliver the fonts, your browser sends your IP address to Google. See the Google privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies. While the site is password-protected, it uses your browser's local storage only to remember that you have entered the password on your device. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Information you send to us

If you email us or submit a form linked from this site, we receive the information you choose to provide. We use it only to answer your enquiry, review applications or coordinate collaborations. Our application forms run on Airtable, a service of Formagrid, Inc. (Airtable) in the USA, which stores your answers on our behalf.

Retention

We keep emails and applications only as long as we need them for these purposes.

Disclosure abroad

GitHub, Google and Airtable process data in the USA. Where required, these transfers rely on recognised safeguards such as the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

External links

This site links to external websites, such as papers, ETH Zürich pages and researchers' personal sites. Their own data protection notices apply once you open them.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. You can also object to its processing. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about data protection on this website can be sent to team@solaris-research.org.
