We are looking for technically strong and safety-driven collaborators. If you want your work to matter, get in touch.

## Thesis projects
Work on real frontier safety problems, co-supervised across ETH labs and industry partners, toward conference publications and reusable open-source artefacts.

## Semester projects
Focused research stretches scoped to produce something concrete: a benchmark, a method, a result worth publishing.

## Independent projects
If you have a safety research question you want to explore, we can support you with compute, mentorship, and feedback. We can also help you find collaborators if you want to build a team around your idea.

## Industry partnerships
If you deploy models and care about what happens to safety after fine-tuning, work with us to discover problems early and ship safer adaptation.
