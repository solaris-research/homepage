Solaris Research

Advancing safety science of frontier models.

Mission

Solaris Research is based at ETH Zürich. Our work is technical: we investigate the internals of frontier models and the conditions under which their safety properties hold or break down. The questions are narrow; the stakes are not.

Research directions

The safety gap. Safety properties in frontier models are not fixed features; they are fragile representations that can shift, weaken, or invert under adaptation. We focus on making this gap visible and controllable: understanding how safety-relevant structure forms inside models, how it changes across pretraining, fine-tuning, and deployment, and when it breaks.

Active interpretability. Most mechanistic tools are applied after training, as diagnostics. We want to bring them earlier; into the training process itself, where they can catch problems before they consolidate. The goal is not only to explain failures but to steer against them in time.

Agentic alignment. The hardest version of this problem is agentic. When a model calls tools, spawns subagents, and executes code across extended horizons, correlated failures compound in ways single-turn evaluations cannot surface. Safety is not solved in a chat window; it is solved when a model remains trustworthy across an autonomous task.

We work on

  • How safety-relevant representations form and degrade across pretraining, fine-tuning, and adaptation; and what predicts drift before it reaches deployment.
  • Whether interpretability can be applied inside the training loop, early enough to catch and correct problems before they are baked in.
  • What standard evaluations miss; the gap between how a model behaves in testing and how it behaves once adapted, deployed, or composed into a larger system.
  • How tool use, multi-agent coordination, and long-horizon action change the safety picture; studied in realistic settings, not by analogy to chat.
  • Reproducible benchmarks, open evaluation pipelines, and mechanism-informed mitigations that others can check, build on, and extend.
